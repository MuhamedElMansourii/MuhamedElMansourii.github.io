About

DUBAI · GMT+4

I'm Mohamed, a video editor based in Dubai, and I have been cutting video for seven years. I learned on my own channels, which reached more than 30 million views and 700K subscribers, so I cut for the viewer who is about to swipe away.

Today most of my work has AI in it. I run @moo4future, a page about AI tools for video, and every tool on my bench was tested there first. When a shot does not exist, I generate it. When an idea is hard to film, I animate it.

Since May 2026 I also edit the reels on Mohammed Aboulfaraj's page and his Tabaqat Podcast: 135 reels and 9.7 million views on his page so far. Arabic is my first language, and I work in Arabic and English.
